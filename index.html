I miss you.

My wifey, I’ve been missing you a lot. I miss talking to you, hearing about your day, laughing over little things, and feeling close to you even when we’re miles apart. Sometimes I wish I could just look into your eyes, hold your hand, and let you understand everything my words don't explain well.

I know things haven't always felt the way they should between us. I know I've hurt you, and I understand that love can't be repaired by one letter or a beautiful website. You deserve care you can feel, honesty you can trust, and effort that doesn't disappear after a difficult conversation.

I'm not asking you to forget what happened or pretend you're no longer hurt. I just want you to know that my feelings are real. I want to learn how to love you better, to make time for us, to listen more carefully, and to show up in the ordinary moments—not only when I'm afraid of losing you.

“I don't just dream of a day with you.
I dream of a whole life beside you.”

I still hope that one day I'll get to look into your eyes in person, take your hand, and marry you. I imagine us building a home full of patience, laughter, kindness, prayer, and the little things that make life ours. And above everything, I want God to be at the centre—not just in the promises we make, but in the way we love, forgive, and care for each other.

I would love for us to begin again, gently. Not by pretending the past didn't happen, but by learning from it and creating something healthier together, one day at a time. I know I have to earn trust through what I do, and I want to take that seriously.

You don't have to give me an answer just because I made this. Take your time and be honest with me. Your feelings matter, too.

With all my heart,
the boy who still hopes to build a future with you. 🤍
